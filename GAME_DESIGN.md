# Tectra Glassbloom: design notes

**Fantasy:** a glassblower's apprentice in a warm, dark hot shop.

**Control:** one finger. Holding breathes into the pipe: in Amber the glass swells at a steady rate (with a short ramp), and in Cobalt and Opaline breathing builds momentum that must be eased off early. The glass rises at a steady pull (faster near the lip on later pieces), and each slice freezes into the vase.

**Scoring:** fidelity is the average closeness to the outline (zero beyond 15 units off). Beads are awarded at per-piece thresholds, tuned so a person deciding every 0.12 s can earn three. The piece bursts at radius 96 and seals at 5.

**Ready state:** the pull waits for the first touch, so nobody loses a piece while reading.

**Reward:** the glass cools from white-hot to its colour before the result appears, and the best piece is kept on the gallery shelf.
